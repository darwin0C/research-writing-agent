# Issues · u03
| # | Sentence (≤12 words, quoted) | Type | Why | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Our results clearly demonstrate that the hydrophilic surface significantly accelerates nucleation" | overclaim | "clearly demonstrate" is rhetorical; "significantly" implies statistics that are not given. | Report the change with numbers only (onset time and percentage reduction); remove "clearly" and "significantly". |
| 2 | "the hydrophilic surface significantly accelerates nucleation" | underspecified | Three contact angles are listed but "the hydrophilic surface" does not say which (35° or 70°), nor which angle gives 350 ps vs 125 ps; fluid/wall system not stated. | Name the contact angles for each onset time (e.g. "from 350 ps at θ = 110° to 125 ps at θ = 35°") and the fluid–wall pair if not stated earlier. |
| 3 | "which proves that the onset time varies monotonically with" | overclaim | Two endpoint values (three angles at most) cannot prove a monotonic trend; "proves" is too strong. | Hedge ("suggests/indicates") and give the 70° value so the trend across all three angles is visible. |
| 4 | "the onset time decreases from 350 ps to 125 ps" | clarity | Observation in a Results section should be past tense per journal style ("Fig. 1 presents" is fine as present). | Use "decreased". |
| 5 | "Our results clearly demonstrate … which proves that the onset" | clarity | One long sentence chains claim, data and inference via a colon and "which". | Split: state the data (with conditions) in one sentence, the hedged trend in the next. |
