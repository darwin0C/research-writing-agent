Fig.~⟦REF1⟧ presents the temporal evolution of the vapor fraction near the wall for contact angles of 35⟦MATH1⟧, 70⟦MATH2⟧ and 110⟦MATH3⟧. Our results clearly demonstrate that the hydrophilic surface significantly accelerates nucleation: the onset time decreases from 350 ps on the 110⟦MATH4⟧ surface to 125 ps on the 35⟦MATH5⟧ surface at a wall temperature of 300 K. Interestingly, the 70⟦MATH6⟧ surface exhibits an intermediate onset time of 210 ps, which proves that the onset time varies monotonically with the contact angle.
