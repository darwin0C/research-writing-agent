Fig.~⟦REF1⟧ shows the temporal evolution of the vapor fraction near the wall for contact angles of 35⟦MATH1⟧, 70⟦MATH2⟧ and 110⟦MATH3⟧. At a wall temperature of 300 K, the onset time of nucleation decreased from 350 ps on the 110⟦MATH4⟧ surface to 125 ps on the 35⟦MATH5⟧ surface, which indicates that a smaller contact angle accelerates nucleation. The 70⟦MATH6⟧ surface exhibited an intermediate onset time of 210 ps. These results are consistent with a monotonic variation of the onset time with the contact angle over the range studied.
