Results and discussion

In recent years, the nucleation behavior of liquids on solid surfaces has garnered significant attention. It is worth noting that the wettability of the wall plays a pivotal role in determining both the nucleation onset and the subsequent bubble growth ⟦CITE1⟧.

⟦REF1⟧ presents the vapor fraction near the wall for contact angles ⟦FMT1⟧ of 35°, 70° and 110°. Our results clearly demonstrate that the hydrophilic surface significantly accelerates nucleation: the onset time decreases from 350 ps to 125 ps at a wall temperature of 300 K and a heat flux of 2 MW/m⟦FMT2⟧, which proves that the onset time varies monotonically with the contact angle.
