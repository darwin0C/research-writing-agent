The increase in nucleation onset time with contact angle can be interpreted using classical nucleation theory, as expressed in Eq.~⟦REF1⟧. Within this framework, the trend may be attributed to a lower energy barrier ⟦MATH1⟧ on more hydrophilic surfaces, resulting from the enhanced solid--liquid interaction ⟦CITE1⟧. This result suggests that interfacial wettability affects the onset of phase change and that, under the conditions studied here, wettability could be considered in the design of cooling surfaces for electronic devices.
