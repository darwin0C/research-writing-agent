Nanostructured surfaces can enhance boiling heat transfer ⟦CITE1⟧, and surface wettability strongly affects bubble nucleation. In this study, molecular dynamics simulations were performed for argon on copper walls with contact angles of 35⟦MATH1⟧ and 110⟦MATH2⟧. At a wall temperature of 300 K, the hydrophilic surface reduced the onset time of nucleation from 350 ps to 125 ps (Fig.~⟦REF1⟧). These results relate surface wettability to nucleation onset, which is relevant to the design of cooling surfaces.
