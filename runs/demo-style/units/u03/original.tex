Fig.~\ref{fig:onset} presents the temporal evolution of the vapor fraction near the wall for contact angles of 35$^\circ$, 70$^\circ$ and 110$^\circ$. Our results clearly demonstrate that the hydrophilic surface significantly accelerates nucleation: the onset time decreases from 350 ps on the 110$^\circ$ surface to 125 ps on the 35$^\circ$ surface at a wall temperature of 300 K. Interestingly, the 70$^\circ$ surface exhibits an intermediate onset time of 210 ps, which proves that the onset time varies monotonically with the contact angle.
