In recent years, nanostructured surfaces have attracted increasing attention due to their remarkable ability to significantly enhance boiling heat transfer \cite{kim2015,chen2019}. It is worth noting that the wettability of the surface plays a crucial role in bubble nucleation. In this study, molecular dynamics simulations were performed for argon on copper walls with contact angles of 35$^\circ$ and 110$^\circ$. Our results clearly demonstrate that the hydrophilic surface reduces the onset time of nucleation from 350 ps to 125 ps when the wall temperature is 300 K, as shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:onset}. This innovative study provides valuable insights into the underlying mechanisms and paves the way for the design of high-performance cooling devices.
