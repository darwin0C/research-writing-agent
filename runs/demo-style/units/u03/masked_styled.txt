Figure~⟦REF1⟧ shows how the vapor fraction near the wall evolves in time. We compare contact angles of 35⟦MATH1⟧, 70⟦MATH2⟧ and 110⟦MATH3⟧. At a wall temperature of 300 K, the onset time of nucleation decreased from 350 ps on the 110⟦MATH4⟧ surface to 125 ps on the 35⟦MATH5⟧ surface. This indicates that a smaller contact angle accelerates nucleation. The 70⟦MATH6⟧ surface gave an intermediate onset time of 210 ps. These results are consistent with the onset time varying monotonically with the contact angle over the range we studied.
